About LittleSproutPlayCo. 🌱

As an educator and mom, I've seen firsthand how meaningful hands-on, sensory play can be for little ones. I also know how busy life can get- and sometimes you just want an easy activity without spending an hour gathering and preparing everything.

That's why I created LittleSproutPlayCo.- to make sensory play easy for busy parents like myself.

Each sensory experience is thoughtfully put together to encourage little hands to scoop, pour, discover, and play. 💛

Less prep. More play. More little moments to grow.
